Brand Background

Hadara was created from a combination of personal experience, passion for fashion, education, and practical industry exposure. Growing up, founder Hadassah Tshaka had trouble finding clothing that was both fashionable and sufficiently modest. Much of the mainstream market prioritises revealing silhouettes and trends that may not align with the preferences of Christian women.

Hadara responds to this gap by demonstrating that modesty does not mean sacrificing elegance, femininity, or style.

Founder & Designer

Hadassah Tshaka is studying Retail Management at the University of Pretoria, building an academic foundation in retail business and commercial fashion. She gained practical industry experience working with Gerrit Pienaar, owner of Claris, developing her understanding of garment production.

Core Brand Values

  • Modesty: Garments providing appropriate coverage without compromising style.
  • Elegance: Polished, sophisticated, and feminine designs.
  • Faith: Supporting Christian women in dressing according to their beliefs.
  • Quality: Carefully designed and constructed garments with fine attention to detail.
  • Femininity: Graceful silhouettes, beautiful fabrics, and elegant details.
